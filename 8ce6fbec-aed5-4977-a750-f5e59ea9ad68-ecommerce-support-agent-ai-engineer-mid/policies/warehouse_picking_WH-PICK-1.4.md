# Larkfield Home - Warehouse picking and tote control
Document: WH-PICK-1.4 | Version 1.4 | Effective 2026-08-15 | Owner: Warehouse Operations

## Scope
Internal instructions for warehouse pickers and shift supervisors during outbound fulfilment. This document does not set Customer Care return, refund or approval rules.

## Picking procedure
Scan the bin location before placing each item in a tote. Keep fragile glassware in a separate compartment. A failed scan goes to the shift supervisor for a stock check. Do not replace a missing SKU with a similar item.

## Tote reconciliation
A tote may remain in staging for 48 hours before the shift supervisor investigates. Record damaged stock against the warehouse loss register at its stock cost, excluding retail margin. Release outbound totes only after the second count matches the pick list. These stock adjustments are not customer payments.
