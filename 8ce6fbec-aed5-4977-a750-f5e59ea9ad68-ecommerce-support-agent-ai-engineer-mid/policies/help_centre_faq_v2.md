# Larkfield Help Centre - Returns & Refunds FAQ
Document: HC-FAQ | Version 2 | Published 2025-10-14 | Owner: Digital Content

**How long do I have to return something?** You can return any item within 14 days of delivery.

**How fast are refunds?** Refunds under $250 are processed automatically as soon as we receive your request. Larger refunds are reviewed by our team.

**My parcel is late.** Most parcels arrive within 7 days. If tracking has not updated for 5 days, contact us and we will look into it.

**Can I get a refund for a damaged item?** Yes - send us a photo and we will refund the item.
